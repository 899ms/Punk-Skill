# Punk Cover Style Catalog

Use these user-visible style names. Do not show excluded photo, avatar, portrait, or pet styles in the menu.

| Style | Template | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 黑白极简概念 | `templates/black-white-minimal-concept.md` | Abstract, editorial, philosophical, strategic, restrained covers with strong typography and visual metaphor. |
| 语义转译极简 | `templates/semantic-minimal-translation.md` | One word, short phrase, slogan, or concept that needs a clever minimal visual translation. |
| 复古手撕拼贴 | `templates/retro-torn-collage.md` | Social posts, cultural topics, controversy, growth, street energy, retro editorial covers. |
| 方块世界 | `templates/block-world.md` | Playful tutorials, tools, systems, building, upgrade, learning, game-like or constructive themes. |
| 巨型透视中文标题 | `templates/giant-perspective-chinese-title.md` | Chinese title-led covers needing maximum impact, spatial depth, speed, conflict, or event-poster energy. |
| 积木世界 | `templates/brick-world.md` | Playful systems, building, teamwork, plans, toys, family, education, and constructive metaphors. |
| 咨询报告视觉 | `templates/consulting-report-visual.md` | Business strategy, frameworks, operations, product thinking, consulting reports, PPT covers, structured analysis. |
| 科研期刊概念 | `templates/research-journal-concept.md` | Science, research, medicine, materials, mechanisms, academic or lab-themed covers. |
| 复古弥散渐变 | `templates/retro-diffuse-gradient.md` | Art, design, music, brand, emotion, atmospheric essays, independent magazine-style covers. |
| 商业杂志头版 | `templates/business-magazine-front-page.md` | Business, technology, AI, startups, investment, trend analysis, sharp magazine-like editorial covers. |
| 黑白灰先锋几何 | `templates/black-white-gray-avant-geometry.md` | Experimental, stark, geometric, modernist, poster-like covers with restrained color. |

## Automatic Recommendations

- For Xiaohongshu tutorials, prefer `巨型透视中文标题`, `复古手撕拼贴`, `方块世界`, or `积木世界`.
- For WeChat public account explainers, prefer `商业杂志头版`, `咨询报告视觉`, `黑白极简概念`, or `复古弥散渐变`.
- For X covers, prefer `商业杂志头版`, `黑白极简概念`, `黑白灰先锋几何`, or `语义转译极简`.
- For research-heavy material, prefer `科研期刊概念`.
- For a single abstract term, prefer `语义转译极简` or `黑白极简概念`.
